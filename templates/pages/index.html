FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files stored on your servers?

No. Imigi operates entirely on client-side technology. Your files stay within your browser window and are never uploaded to an external server.

What file formats are supported?

We support standard formats including JPG, PNG, WEBP, and PDF, as well as camera-based QR codes and document scanning capabilities.

Can I scan documents on mobile devices?

Yes. The built-in document scanner utilizes WebRTC to access modern browser cameras on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

Is installation or software setup required?

No software installation is required. You can use Imigi directly in any web browser or install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for offline access.
